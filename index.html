Where your prospects come from

Sora does not provide a contact database. We are not Apollo, ZoomInfo or a contact finder, and we do not supply lists of people or their contact details.

You source your own prospects using external platforms such as Apollo or LinkedIn, your existing database, referrals or any other lead source. You then bring those prospects into Sora to research, organise, approach and manage them.
