Common Questions

Everything you need to know

The questions we get asked most before a strategy call.

How is this different from hiring a salesperson?
Hiring a salesperson takes 3–4 months to recruit, costs £50–70k in salary alone — before you add NI, benefits, tools and management time — and takes another 3–6 months to ramp before you see results. With Sora Collective, you have a fully operational outbound sales function running within 30 days, at a fraction of the cost, with no long-term employment risk. If something isn't working, we adapt immediately. There is no equivalent flexibility with a hire.
How quickly will we see results?
Most clients see their first qualified meetings booked within 30 days of onboarding. Pipeline growth — measured by the volume and quality of opportunities in your CRM — is typically visible within 60–90 days. The exact timeline depends on your market, your offer and the complexity of your sales cycle, which is why we scope this clearly before you commit to anything.
What industries do you work with?
We work exclusively with B2B companies. Our clients span technology and SaaS, professional services, financial services, healthcare, real estate, recruitment, manufacturing and legal services. The common thread is not the industry — it is the profile. We work best with established businesses that have a proven product and are ready to scale their sales operation seriously.
What does it actually cost?
Pricing varies based on the scope of service, team size and market. We design every engagement specifically for the client — we do not offer off-the-shelf packages. What we will say is that our outsourced sales service consistently costs significantly less than the equivalent in-house hire once you factor in the full cost of employment. We are transparent about pricing from the first conversation.
Do we need to be involved day-to-day?
No. That is the point. We handle the daily execution — prospecting, outreach, follow-up and pipeline management. You receive weekly reports and join a monthly strategy review. Outside of that, your involvement is minimal. We are designed to free up your time, not consume more of it.
What do you need from us to get started?
A clear product or service, a defined target market and access to a CRM (or willingness to set one up). We handle the rest — strategy, messaging, outreach, reporting. The onboarding process involves a thorough briefing from your side so we can represent your business accurately and credibly in market. Most clients are surprised by how light-touch the setup is on their end.
