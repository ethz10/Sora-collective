Sora Collective ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we use it when you visit our website or apply to the Founder Programme.

1. Who We Are

Sora Collective operates a software business launch programme helping individuals launch their own branded software companies. We are based in Dubai, UAE with operations in London, UK and New York, USA.

For the purposes of applicable data protection legislation — including the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL), the UK GDPR and the EU GDPR where relevant — Sora Collective is the data controller.

Contact us at info@soracollective.co with any questions about this policy.

2. What We Collect

When you apply to the Founder Programme or contact us, we collect:

  • Identity data — first name, last name, age range
  • Contact data — email address, phone number, country
  • Application data — employment status, sales experience, motivations, time commitment, goals, programme of interest
  • Technical data — IP address, browser type, device information, operating system
  • Usage data — pages visited, time on page, links clicked

We do not collect special category data such as health information, religious beliefs or ethnicity.

3. Why We Collect It

  • To assess your application — we review every application individually (Legal basis: legitimate interests / pre-contract steps)
  • To contact you about your application and arrange a discovery call (Legal basis: legitimate interests)
  • To deliver the programme if you are accepted (Legal basis: contract performance)
  • To improve our website through analytics (Legal basis: legitimate interests)
  • To send relevant updates where you have opted in (Legal basis: consent)
  • To meet legal obligations (Legal basis: legal obligation)

4. Cookies

We use cookies to make the site work and to understand how it is used. These fall into three categories:

  • Essential — required for the site to function
  • Analytics — help us measure traffic and improve the experience
  • Functional — remember your preferences

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time.

5. Who We Share It With

We share data only where necessary:

  • Service providers — CRM, email, scheduling and analytics platforms, all bound by data processing agreements
  • Professional advisers — lawyers and accountants where required
  • Authorities — where legally obliged

We never sell, rent or trade your personal data.

6. International Transfers

As we operate across the UAE, UK and USA, your data may be transferred between these jurisdictions. Where this happens, we apply appropriate safeguards including Standard Contractual Clauses where required.

7. How Long We Keep It

  • Unsuccessful applications — 12 months from submission
  • Accepted founders — duration of the programme plus 7 years for accounting purposes
  • General enquiries — 24 months from last contact
  • Analytics data — up to 26 months

After these periods, data is securely deleted or anonymised.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Have inaccurate data corrected
  • Request deletion of your data in certain circumstances
  • Restrict how we process your data
  • Receive your data in a portable format
  • Object to processing, including direct marketing
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise any of these, email info@soracollective.co. We respond within 30 days. You may also complain to the UAE Data Office or, in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

9. Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data against loss, unauthorised access or disclosure. Access is limited to those with a legitimate business need.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted here with a revised date. We encourage you to review it periodically.
