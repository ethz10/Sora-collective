What makes an opportunity qualified?

Before a meeting reaches your sales calendar, we aim to establish five things.

01 — Need

Need

Is there a genuine business problem or requirement?

02 — Relevance

Relevance

Could your product or service realistically solve it?

03 — Authority

Authority

Are we speaking with the right person, or someone who can influence the decision?

04 — Timing

Timing

Is there a reason to explore the solution now?

05 — Intent

Intent

Has the prospect genuinely agreed to have a sales conversation?

A reply is not a lead. A calendar booking is not automatically an opportunity.

We qualify the conversation before it reaches your sales team.
